# The World Today — Sunday, 22 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Sunday, 22 November 2026, drawn
as a newspaper front page. The program copies the bundled image to
`/output/entry.png`.

## The stories in the image

- **Lead — Gulf escalation:** Iran strikes a US air base in Kuwait and
  targets Bahrain; several tankers are hit off the Oman coast; an ADNOC
  vessel is attacked in the Strait of Hormuz, with one crew member missing.
- **Peru:** a magnitude 6.9 earthquake shakes the Andes; no casualties
  were immediately reported.
- **Baltic:** drones hit a Russian cargo ship; Finland tightens security
  and Estonia moves to close its border with Russia.
- **Mexico:** cartel kingpin "El Chapo Isidro" is captured by Mexican
  forces; deadly cartel violence erupts in Sinaloa, leaving 11 dead.
- **Summit:** Trump reaches Shenzhen for APEC; a trilateral meeting with
  Xi and Putin is planned; Zelensky calls Putin a "killer" after Trump
  calls him a friend.
- **Climate:** COP31 talks in Antalya are deadlocked over fossil fuels.
- **Europe:** Russia launches a huge air assault on Ukraine — 400 drones
  and 110 missiles — while Russian missiles and a drone crash in Poland
  and Warsaw flags a provocation.
- **Brazil:** imprisoned former president Jair Bolsonaro is admitted to
  intensive care with an acute bowel obstruction.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG). Python 3.13, standard library only.
